About us

Your Local Towing Crew Since 2006

Eagle 24 Hour Towing was established in 2006 by Renier, who saw a growing gap in the Hartbeespoort area. With tourists, growing communities and busy roads, the area needed a towing and roadside assistance service based right here — one that treats every client like a neighbour.

Today we operate a fully equipped fleet covering accident assistance, breakdowns, long-distance towing, vehicle recoveries and secure storage — backed by a BEE accredited business and a code of conduct our drivers live by.

  • Established 2006 — trusted for 20 years
  • BEE accredited, registered operator
  • Secured storage facility with 24-hour security
  • Based in Melodie, Hartbeespoort
