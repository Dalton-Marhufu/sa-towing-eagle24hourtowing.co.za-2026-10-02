## Top 3 Issues
- Homepage leads with a company-history essay and keyword-stuffed title instead of a clear emergency offer, so visitors don't know what to do first.
- Services are buried on a separate "Our Services" content page with a bare bullet list and zero photos, CTAs or urgency.
- Contact page says "WhatsApp us now" but no working WhatsApp link exists anywhere; the only conversion path is a scattered set of tel: links with no consistent CTA or form.

## Top 3 Improvements
- Rebuilt as a single high-conversion landing page: 24/7 emergency hero with real fleet photos, prominent "Call Now" and WhatsApp buttons above the fold.
- All 15 exact services presented in a visual card grid with icons and descriptions, so the full offer is scannable in seconds.
- Added a callback request form that routes straight to WhatsApp, plus a persistent top bar, sticky header and quick-contact CTA that stay visible while scrolling.
